Why the chat feels busy

  1. Your turns don't register. Your bubble is --bg-card #f0f0e8 on the panel's #f5f5f0, about a 2% difference. On a phone it reads as no bubble, so your words and hers merge into one column.
  2. Both voices use the same typeface at nearly the same size (serif, 1.05rem vs 0.98rem). Nothing tells you who is speaking until you read the words.
  3. 間 is flattened. At line-height 1.95 every line already carries about 1rem of air. The 0.55rem gap between paragraphs is smaller than that, and the 2.2rem gap between speakers is only about one line. When every gap looks the same, nothing groups (§1). Claude Code reads calmer at roughly 1.6 line-height with clearly bigger gaps between turns.
  4. Three textures inside one turn: serif Japanese, an English aside in sans with a blue rule, then more serif. Each aside breaks her turn into separate blocks.

Round 2 (you picked A): is it the font? Shippori Mincho 400 has hairline strokes, and Japanese is set on a full-width grid, so every kana gets the same wide box. A1–A4 keep A's spacing and try a heavier weight, proportional kana (palt) and sturdier faces. Finding: palt does nothing in Shippori Mincho (A1, A2) or Zen Kaku Gothic (A4) as Google serves them; only Noto Serif JP (A3) really tightens. A2b tightens Shippori by hand instead. The controls still tune spacing for all of them.
