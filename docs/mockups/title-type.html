What to look for

  1. 1
    Does it still read as a page title? The active word should hold the heading on its own, like 復習 or 今日 do. Treatments where the siblings compete (3, 4, 5) make the row read as a menu instead.
  2. 2
    Is the other page obviously tappable? The siblings have no button styling, so they need to look like a quiet, deliberate part of the heading, not leftover text. Size alone (1, 2, 12) or position (8, 9) can carry that.
  3. 3
    Japanese vs. English. Kanji titles are dense, and at small sizes 本棚 and 番組 stay legible longer than katakana ニュース does. Check every specimen in both languages, and with three pages.
  4. 4
    Motion. Size-based treatments (1, 2, 7, 11, 12) visibly grow and shrink when you switch. Tone-based ones (3, 4, 5, 10) just crossfade, which is calmer. 8 and 9 move the active title to the left.
  5. 5
    Consistency with the other tabs. 今日 and 復習 keep a single 2rem title. A treatment that keeps the active title at 2rem (most of these) keeps the headings consistent as you move between tabs.
