Progress: time, listening, reading, chat

Three ways to add activity stats above what 進捗 already shows. Each phone switches between this week, this month and all time; the toggle inside any phone moves all three. The dimmed card under each is the existing review heatmap, unchanged, and the JLPT donuts and word list follow it as today.

What the numbers need. Articles opened, words looked up and reviews go back months already. Some numbers are new:
  • Time in app isn't tracked anywhere yet. It needs a small daily row counting the seconds the app is on screen, and, for B and C's split, the seconds spent in the decks and with ハルカ.
  • Listening minutes today are only the length of podcasts you finished. Real minutes need the podcast and song players to count playback into the same row.
  • Chat turns can't come from transcripts, which are deleted after 30 days. The daily row keeps a count.
  • Reading minutes, articles finished and song plays started being recorded on 17 Oct. In “All time”, those numbers say “since 17 Oct”.
