Playlists, kept simple

Four ways to add playlists, from no lists at all to named lists you make yourself. All four share one player change: when a song ends, the next one starts, and the lyrics end with what's coming next. The STATE toggle moves every phone through the same four moments. Phones also respond to taps: the お気に入り slider, the list shelves, the chips and the pick rows.

STATE

What's the same in all four

  1. 1
    The player keeps going. Today a song stops at its end. With a queue, the next song starts after a short handoff (cover, title, a 3-second ring, and 止める to stay put). The lyrics end with ♪ and then 次の曲: the next song as a row, and the one after it fainter. Tap a row to jump to it.
  2. 2
    The position sits where a card's status goes: 3 / 8 at the right of the pinned back row (§6.5's "where you are, nothing more"). The context (お気に入り, 夜のドライブ, シティポップ) is the small label under the title, where the style is now.
  3. 3
    No new transport buttons. ⟳10 stays ⟳10. Skipping a song = tap 次の曲 at the end of the lyrics. リピート cycles リピート → 1曲 → 全部: off, loop this song, loop the whole queue.
  4. 4
    One way to start a queue: ▶ 通して聴く (white dot, serif label) plays what's on screen, in order. シャッフル beside it is a quiet text link. Tapping a single cover still plays that one song, then carries on through the rest of the view, like any music app.
  5. 5
    Open question: leaving the player. Today leaving the player pauses the song, because there's no mini player. A queue makes background listening more tempting (cooking, a walk). That's a separate change: a slim now-playing row above the nav. I'd keep it out of this round unless you want it.

Recommendation

  1. →
    Option 2, with option 1's player. One list you build with a tap is all most people need for a personal library of generated songs, especially while it's still a few dozen songs. It adds one compact slider and one ♡, and nothing to name or organise. If it later outgrows that, option 3's shelf can sit above the grid with お気に入り as its first list, so nothing built for 2 is thrown away.
  2. →
    Option 4's style chips are a cheap add-on to any of them if the grid gets long. I'd hold them until the grid passes about 20 songs, since with few songs most chips would hold one or two.
