A ring around the length

The corner's “2 / 5” and “4 / 12分” become just the total, 5 or 12, with a thin ring around it that fills clockwise as you read or listen and closes at the end. Once it's finished, the ring shifts shade. Four takes on how quiet the ring is and what “finished” looks like. The top card on each phone follows the slider; press Play to watch it fill and close.

What the variants try

  1. 1
    Ink. The arc is the muted ink at half strength over the hairline track, the same weight as the old text. Finished: the ring closes and settles into the soft blue tint, and the number fades back. The quietest change of shade: “done” recedes.
  2. 2
    Slate. The arc is --accent-progress, the flashcard bar's colour, over the hairline track. Finished: it deepens to the main ink. The clearest progress, but slate on a card is what we pulled back from on the card edge.
  3. 3
    Mist. A slightly wider band in --accent-primary, the same soft blue as the card's progress edge and the nav pill. Finished: it turns slate. Reads as part of the card's colour family rather than a mark on top.
  4. 4
    No track. No grey circle before you start: just the number, as today. The arc draws itself in muted ink as you go. Finished: it closes and turns soft blue. A new item looks exactly like the plain number does now.

Details that apply to all four

  1. 5
    The number is the total, never the position. 本棚: parts (5). 番組: minutes (12). The ring carries where you are: overall progress through the work (the same 0–1 the しおり saves) or the listen position. The UNIT toggle tries the unit three ways: None (just the number), Beside (部 / 分 in small muted type just after the ring, outside it) and Inside (the ring grows from 26 to 32px so “12分” fits inside, the unit smaller than the number).
  2. 6
    Single-part works get a ring too. Today a one-part work shows nothing in the corner. With a ring, “1” fills as you read it, so every work shows how far along you are. (The card with 1 below shows this.)
  3. 7
    Same ring on 今日. The continue-reading card on 今日 uses the compact size (22px vs 26px), matching its smaller labels.
  4. 8
    Motion only when it changes. The ring eases to its new length when you come back from the reader or player, and the shade shift fades in over about 0.8s. No looping or pulsing.
