本棚 and 番組 cards, as built

The archive cards now match the news cards: same padding, tag, title size and plain top-right status, with nothing under the title (no meta line, no progress bar). Long-form works drop the tag; their part count sits in the corner. Podcasts show minutes heard out of the total. Each pair is before, then after.

What changed

  1. 1Card style = news card. Padding 1.5rem, tag 0.65rem, title 1.35rem. The daily digest no longer gets the white card or the blue tag; every card uses the same fill.
  2. 2Nothing under the title. Gone: 約N字 · 全N部 · date on works; host × guest · dialect · length · date on episodes; the 3px progress bar on both.
  3. 3本棚 top-right: 2 / 4, the part you're on out of the total (3 / 3 once finished). A single-part work shows nothing. No tag on works, so the count floats beside the title.
  4. 4番組 top-right: 4 / 12分 while listening, 12分 before you start, 15 / 15分 once heard. The "being made" spinner and 再試行 stay.
