The title is the switch

This replaces the ニュース | 本棚 pill under 読む's heading with a heading that switches pages itself. The same mechanism carries to 聞く (Podcasts | Music). All three phones are live: tap a title, swipe the page sideways (A and C), and use the nav to move between 読む and 聞く.

LABELS
A · TITLES SIDE BY SIDE
The active page is the 2rem title. Siblings sit on the same baseline, smaller and muted. Tap one, or swipe the page, and they trade places.
B · TITLE MENU
A chevron beside the title opens a small list. It takes the least room and scales to any number of pages, but the other pages are hidden.
C · TITLE STRIP
All titles are full size in one row, with the next one fading at the edge. Swiping slides the titles with the page. Dots show where you are.

Things to decide either way

  1. 1
    The tab name leaves the page. 読む and 聞く stay in the nav, but the page title becomes ニュース / 本棚 or ポッドキャスト / 音楽. That's the same pattern 復習 | 進捗 could move to later, which would remove the last pill from tab pages.
  2. 2
    Remember the last page per tab. 読む reopens on whichever of News or Library you left, and 今日's blocks still deep-link (すべて → News, 続きを読む → Library), as they do now.
  3. 3
    Swipe needs care on 読む → News. Feed cards already swipe left to reveal SAVE / DELETE. Page swipes would only start from the heading or the empty margins, or need a longer horizontal travel than a card swipe.
  4. 4
    Actions follow the page. + shows on Library and Podcasts only, which is the same rule as now.

Recommendation: A. It keeps both names visible, switches in one tap, leaves the title's word-card tap alone, and fits the two-page tabs we're planning. B is the fallback if a tab ever needs four or more pages.
