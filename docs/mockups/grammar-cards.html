Grammar cards from the weekly recap, mixed into the 単語 session, and the recap list at the bottom of 進捗. The phones are clickable: pick a choice to flip the card, then tap the card (or a grade) to move on. The sentences come from this week's real recap (Oct 19–25); a vocab card sits between them as in a real session.

1 · CHOICES UNDER THE SENTENCE
Two pills under the sentence. Big, easy targets, and the sentence stays whole to read. The blank is a plain line.
2 · CHOICES IN THE BLANK
The two choices stacked in the gap. You read them in place, but long choices (勝つチャンス / 勝つのチャンス) push the sentence apart and the targets are smaller.
3 · 進捗 · WEEKLY RECAPS
The bottom of 進捗, scrolled down. One row per week, newest first. A tap opens the same sheet as in her chat; swipe it down (or tap the bar) to close. The earlier weeks are made up to show the list.

Notes

  1. Grading. A wrong pick starts the slider on もう一度; a right one starts it from your thinking time, as on word cards. One tap moves on: the card keeps the suggested grade, a grade tap slides and commits. Try picking wrong on 旅館＿泊まります.
  2. The back shows the sentence again with the answer filled in and marked (the recap's fix colour), "You picked で" only when you were wrong, and the explanation. No ✓ / ✗ icons, no colours for right and wrong.
  3. The kicker on a grammar card names the recap pattern (PARTICLES WITH VERBS), so it doesn't read as a vocab card. The progress row says GRAMMAR instead of NEW / REVIEW.
  4. A "nothing" choice becomes two whole phrases (勝つチャンス / 勝つのチャンス), never an empty pill.
  5. 進捗: the list sits under the word list and only appears once there's a recap. The NEW tag marks a recap you haven't opened yet; drop it if it's one thing too many.
