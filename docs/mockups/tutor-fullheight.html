Full-height chat

The header goes. Without the ハ / ハルカ / 終わる row the thread runs to the panel's top edge and fades out under the rounded corner. That gains about 70px, roughly two lines of her text. Closing needs no button: swipe back or tap the dim. 終わる needs a new home, since it's what ends the conversation and shows the recap. A and B are the two places it could go; neither adds anything new.

Over the status bar: not worth it. The app runs with iOS's default status bar, an opaque band the page can't draw into. The only way under it is black-translucent, which forces the clock and battery to white (unreadable on our cream) and pushes every page in the app under the clock, not just the chat. In a Safari tab it isn't possible at all.
