Activity Axis

A rough y-axis for the 進捗 minutes card: two or three round marks (30m, 1h, 2h…) so a bar reads as "about an hour", not a precise value. Bars now scale to the round top, not the tallest bar.

A · HAIRLINES

Faint dotted lines across the card at each mark, small labels in a left gutter.

B · ONE LINE

A single quiet line at the round top, its label at the right end; the middle mark is only a label.

C · TICKS ONLY

No lines across: labels on the left with a short tick mark, like a ruler's edge.

Scale rule (all variants): pick a round step from 5m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 2h, 3h, 5h, 10h, 20h… so the tallest bar fits in at most three steps; the top of the plot is the last step. Labels read 15m, 30m, 1h, 2h; never 1h 30m.
