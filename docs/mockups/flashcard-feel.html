MOCKUP · FLASHCARD FEEL

浮かぶカード

Ways to make the study card feel like an object you hold rather than a flat panel, staying in the same calm palette. Every card is live: tap to flip, set the slider, tap the card to move on. The main shared idea is a separate shadow on the "table": as the card rises, that shadow softens and shrinks, which is what makes something look like it floats. V0 is today's card for comparison.
