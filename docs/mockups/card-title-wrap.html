What changed

  1. 1
    Wrap. Before, the title kept 3.5rem clear for the ring on every line, so long titles broke early and left a gap down the right side. Now the ring floats at the title's top right: only the first line gives up room, and the lines below use the full width.
  2. 2
    Three lines. Titles stop at three lines with an ellipsis, on 本棚 and on 番組 (tagged cards). Home's card stays at two.
  3. 3
    No “# ”. Imported titles drop leading Markdown heading marks.
