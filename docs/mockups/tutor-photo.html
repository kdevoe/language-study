What happens to the photo

  • The phone shrinks it to about 1500px on the long side as a JPEG before sending (also turns HEIC into JPEG).
  • ハルカ sees the real image on that one turn. Alongside her reply, Haiku writes the transcript: any text word for word, then a description. A document gets one line; a scene (a hike, a meal) gets two or three sentences with what matters for talking about it: place, season, weather, what stands out.
  • She answers the way the photo asks. A document: she reads it and helps. Something from your life: she reacts like a friend, names what's in it in Japanese, and saves a note ("went hiking on 2026-10-17").
  • Option: a small thumbnail (about 30KB) kept on the phone only, for today's conversation, so a hike photo doesn't turn into a chip on reload. Cleared when the conversation ends.
  • The saved conversation keeps only the transcript, fenced as untrusted text like an article. Later turns, reloads and the overnight memory all read that.
  • Nothing goes to storage: no bucket, no migration, no photo cleanup.
