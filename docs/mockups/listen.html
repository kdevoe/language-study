What this demonstrates

  1. 1
    LISTEN takes SETTINGS' slot: NEWS · LIBRARY · LISTEN · REVIEW · PROGRESS. The rail keeps today's lucide icons (LISTEN = Headphones). Settings stays in the header gear, and STUDY is relabelled REVIEW.
  2. 2
    Same card language as the Library. Today's DAILY DIGEST sits on top with a white card. The cast rotates per episode and shows on the card (ケンジ × ヨウコ), with the dialect noted when one is present.
  3. 3
    Status mirrors the job: 台本を作成中… → 音声を収録中… → ✓ READY, or ↻ 再試行 on failure. Try the + sheet: the new card walks through each state.Episodes take about a minute (about 50s script + about 30s audio), so the placeholder card matters.
  4. 4
    Transcript as the interface. The active sentence gets a soft olive tint and the current word turns olive. Auto-scroll keeps the active line about 40% down. Scroll away and auto-scroll stops; ↓ 再生位置へ brings you back.
  5. 5
    Tap a word → pause + dictionary (the WordModal). Closing resumes playback. Lookups count toward SRS exactly as in the Reader; there is no passive "heard it" credit. Double-tap a sentence → jump there.
  6. 6
    Transport is for learners: ⏮/⏭ step by sentence, not ±10s. Speed cycles 0.75 / 0.9 / 1.0 / 1.15× with pitch preserved. ふ toggles furigana. 耳で聴く (listen-first) blurs the transcript; tap a sentence to reveal it.
  7. 7
    Mini-player: leave the player while audio is playing and a slim bar stays above the nav on every tab. Lock-screen controls come via the Media Session API. Reopening resumes where you stopped.
