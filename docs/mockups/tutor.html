Opening it

  • Swipe in from the right edge (left in Settings for left-handed use). The touch must start within 18px of the edge, so it never fights card swipes or swipe-to-delete.
  • A faint mark on that edge is the only thing added to every screen. It could fade after the first week.
  • From a lookup: the word panel, grammar panel and sentence (。) sheet end with ハルカに聞く, which opens it focused on that word, point or sentence. Long-press stays with furigana.
  • Fallback for the Safari tab, where iOS owns the edges: drag up on the bottom nav.

What it knows

  • The chip appears only when there's something specific on screen (a sentence, a card, a podcast line) and shows exactly what it took. Tap × and it won't use it. Pages with nothing specific (Home, lists) get no chip.
  • Flashcards: it sees the front only and gives hints, not answers.
  • Audio pauses while it's open and resumes after.
  • From memory: what you read and heard, your HARD grammar points, what you told it before.

How it talks

  • Japanese by default, at your level. Asides (muted, with a blue rule) are the tutor stepping out to explain, then straight back.
  • Tutor text is plain serif, no bubbles; your messages sit in a soft box. Every word is tappable like the reader.
  • Suggestions are quiet text, not buttons. No mode switch: "quiz me", "let's chat", "explain in English" are just things you say.
