What changes

  1. 1
    今日 is the first tab and where the app opens. Each block is a shortcut into another tab, not a copy: the review card starts the deck in 復習, 続きを読む opens the Reader at the しおり, a kanji opens its card, and the podcast plays in 聞く.
  2. 2
    Review leads. It's the one thing that's time-sensitive, so it's the featured white card (§6.5). When nothing is due it shrinks to 何もありません with Discover as the onward action.
  3. 3
    Blocks hide when empty. No work in progress → no 続きを読む. No new podcast → no 聞く row. A quiet day is a short page.
  4. 4
    読む = ニュース | 本棚, in the same segment style as 復習 | 進捗. The 本棚 side is today's archive list unchanged (search, newest month without a heading, older months collapsed).
  5. 5
    Settings lives on 今日 only, top-right where people look for it. The other tabs keep just their title, plus the + on 本棚 and 聞く.
