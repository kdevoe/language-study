Home, 音楽 and the list page (BUILT with the default toggles)

Three phones, one per screen you asked about. 音楽: the ＋ moves from the page heading to the 曲 row, and the リスト / 曲 headings grow. List page: the pill “▶ 再生” becomes a play button in the style of 今日's podcast row. Home: 今日 becomes Home with a house icon, the cards' text is larger, and a new block plays the list you last played. The toggles change the options where there's a real choice.

What changes

  1. 1
    ＋ belongs to 曲. The ＋ leaves the page heading (where it sat beside 番組 | 音楽 and read as “add a list” or “add anything”) and sits at the right end of the 曲 row, so it's clearly “make a song”. Lists keep their own ＋ 新しいリスト tile. When there are no songs yet (no shelf, no 曲 row), the empty state gets a 曲を作る button instead.
  2. 2
    Bigger headings. リスト and 曲 go from 0.7rem to 0.82rem sans. “Serif” tries them as small serif headings (1rem) instead; it's more of a change in character, closer to the page titles.
  3. 3
    Play like Home. A white lifted circle with a filled triangle, the same as Home's podcast row, scaled up to 56px since it's the page's main action. シャッフル becomes a quiet icon beside it. “Pill + icon” keeps the word 再生 next to a real triangle, if you'd rather keep the text.
  4. 4
    Home. The tab is Home with a house icon. As a Japanese label it would be ホーム (what Japanese apps use); the adaptive switch would show it once the word is learned. Text on the cards goes from 1.05rem to 1.2rem (titles) and 0.68rem to 0.74rem (labels); the ring grows to match. “Largest” is 1.3rem.
  5. 5
    Recent list. A new block between the podcast and the news, marked by a music-note icon (which opens 音楽): the list you last played, or the last one you added to if you haven't played one. Tapping the play button plays it from the top; tapping the rest opens the list's page. “Row” matches the podcast row; “Collage card” gives the covers more room.
