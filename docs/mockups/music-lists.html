Making lists, adding songs

How option 3 works in practice. There's one sheet, リストに追加, and every way of adding a song ends there: from the player, from a long-press in the grid, or from a list's own page. You make a list in two places: inside that sheet, already holding the song you're on, or from the shelf's ＋, where you pick songs next. The toggles change the two open choices; pick rows in the sheets are tappable.

PLAYER ENTRY
AFTER A TAP

A · Listening, add this song to a list

The case you described. Three taps at most, and you never leave the song: it keeps playing under the sheet.

B · No list fits: make one right there

Same sheet. ＋ 新しいリスト swaps in place for a name field; 作る creates the list with this song already in it.

C · Start a list from the shelf, then fill it

For building a list on purpose: name it, then pick songs from your covers.

D · From the grid

Long-press a cover. It splits like the お気に入り idea: リストに追加 over 削除. The same sheet opens.

How it behaves

  1. 1
    One sheet for every add. The song at the top, then your lists as pick rows (Settings' pick-list pattern: white with a check = this song is in it). Tapping a white row takes the song out again, so the same sheet adds and removes. Lists are ordered by most recently added to, so the list you used last is always the first row.
  2. 2
    Player entry: a ＋ icon in the pinned back row (Lucide list-plus, §6.7). When the song is in at least one list it darkens to --text-main as a quiet "done" state, the way icons show done elsewhere. Long-press on the cover is invisible (nobody finds it), and a ⋯ menu adds a tap and a whole menu for one action. I'd go with the icon.
  3. 3
    After a tap: the sheet closes about half a second after the row lifts, and a toast names the list with 元に戻す. Most adds go to one list, so one tap finishing the job feels right. With "Sheet stays" it behaves like a checklist: tap several lists, then tap outside. Try both.
  4. 4
    New list from the sheet skips the song picker: the list starts with the song you're on. Below the name field, three suggested names come from the song (its mood, style or theme), as plain serif text (§6.4). No list cover to choose: the shelf builds the 2×2 collage from the first four songs.
  5. 5
    New list from the shelf is a name, then the picker: your covers with a check circle each (the archive's select pattern, §6.8), and 追加 in the action bar with the count. Skipping the picker leaves an empty list whose page says まだ曲がありません with 曲を選ぶ.
  6. 6
    Managing lists stays out of the way. Rename: tap the title on the list's page, and it becomes a hero input in place. Delete a list: long-press it on the shelf, 削除, with the 5s 元に戻す toast (undo, never confirm). Remove a song: swipe left on its row. Reorder: drag the grip. Deleting a song also takes it out of every list.
