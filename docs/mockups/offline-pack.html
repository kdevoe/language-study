MOCKUP · EXPLORATION

Offline, in Settings

One setting keeps a pack on the phone for flights: unread news, the next parts of what you're reading, recent episodes, your songs and the dictionary. It refreshes itself while it's on. The app behaves offline whenever there's no signal, pack or not, so this is the only control. Use the switch to see each state.

STATE

NOTES

  1. Where. Its own section, オフライン, after 表示 and before 詳細: it's about the device, not about studying or reading. One label, one control, like every other setting.
  2. 1 · Minimal. An Off / On slider (compact, like a secondary choice). The only status is the small text at the right of the label, where Text size shows its 18px: the size when ready, "4 / 12" while saving, nothing when off. No hint line.
  3. 2 · With contents. The same, plus one muted line of what's kept (counts), so you can check before boarding that your podcast made it. A thin progress line while saving.
  4. 3 · Small / Large. Off · Small · Large instead of a switch. Small ≈ 100 MB (8 articles, 3 episodes, songs in your lists); Large ≈ 350 MB (15 articles, 6 episodes, 30 most played songs too). Answers "how much" without a separate setting.
  5. Cleared by iOS. iOS can delete a web app's storage when the phone is short of space. The setting stays on and refetches on the next launch with signal; until then the label says 再保存待ち (waiting to save again) rather than a size, so a missing pack is never a surprise mid-flight.
  6. Not shown: nothing anywhere else. No badge on Home, no banner. Offline states inside the app (feed end, grammar note, dimmed create buttons) are the next phase.
