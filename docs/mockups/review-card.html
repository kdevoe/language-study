The 復習 card, five ways

The card at the top of 今日 is too tall for what it says. Phone 0 is how it looks now; 1–5 are shorter takes, each shown in the full page so you can see how it sits above 続きを読む, 聞く and ニュース. Round three, as built: white card, no 新 / 復習 split, and every section label led by its tab's icon (On label). ICON SIZE sets the label icons live; the label text is 11px. Phone 1 is what the app now ships: 19px icons with no label text, and the reading block labelled 本棚 (the app's adaptive label for that page) rather than 続きを読む. The toggles apply to all six phones; ICON tries it inside the card, small in the card's top-left corner, or beside the label.

The five

  1. 1One row. The count on the left at 1.8rem, 始める on the right. 復習 moves out to the section label like every other block. About half the height of now.
  2. 2Number as anchor. Keeps the big 2.6rem number but puts it beside the words instead of above them, so the size stays and the empty band goes.
  3. 3Lifted 始める. The action becomes a white pill with the thumb shadow (the "ready" lift from the design system), so the card itself can sit on the soft fill and the button carries the emphasis.
  4. 4Same shape as 聞く. Title, small meta, round white button on the right: review and listen become siblings, told apart by their button and label. Most uniform, least prominent.
  5. 5One line. Label, count and 始める on a single baseline inside the card. The shortest; with the split gone it no longer crowds at 142.
